# Safety and Scope

Use IAOT only with apps you own or are explicitly authorized to test. The public repository deliberately contains no third-party offsets, private dumps, bypasses, anti-tamper/DRM circumvention, entitlement bypasses, stealth behavior, cheat branding, or jailbreak-only injection.

It targets jailed iOS and does not guarantee App Store compatibility. Users are responsible for authorization, licenses, signing identities, entitlements, device permissions, and applicable law. Do not publish app-specific private material in public examples.
